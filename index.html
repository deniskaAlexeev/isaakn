The Journey.

As an international singer and songwriter, I create music that transcends borders. Performing and writing primarily in English, my sound is an atmospheric blend of modern pop, deep emotion, and stylish aesthetics.

Formerly the lead singer of the acclaimed group Triangle Sun, I have embarked on a profound solo journey, bringing a refined, premium musical experience to stages worldwide.
